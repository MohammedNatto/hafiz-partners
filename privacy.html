الاحتفاظ والحذف

  • التسجيلات الصوتية: لا تُخزَّن أصلاً. لو فُعّل التخزين مستقبلاً فالحد الأقصى 7 أيام ثم حذف آلي.
  • عامل احتفاظ يومي يحذف سجلات التكرار الأقدم من 90 يوماً والأسر التي لم يبقَ لها ولي أمر.
  • حق الحذف: زر «حذف بيانات الطفل نهائياً» في لوحة ولي الأمر يحذف كل بيانات الطفل من الجهاز ومن الخادم فوراً، ويعرض وقت الحذف. حذف الحساب يحذف الأسرة كاملة.
  • الموافقات منفصلة لكل غرض (الدوري، الدقة العالية، حلقة المعلّم) وتُسحب من اللوحة في أي وقت.

لا دردشة، لا تعليقات، لا صداقات، لا مشاركة صوت. الدوري أرقام وأسماء مستعارة فقط. كل شاشات ولي الأمر خلف بوابة والدية.

Privacy Policy — Hafiz (English)

Summary: Hafiz helps children memorise the Quran. The child's voice is processed on the device and erased as soon as the result is extracted. We store no recordings and collect no real name, photo, location or birth date. There are no third-party analytics or ads. Every consent is separate and revocable, and all data can be deleted immediately with one tap.

  • On-device by default. Recitation audio never leaves the device unless the guardian enables the separate "high accuracy" (cloud recognition) consent; then it is processed in memory on our server and discarded with the response — never written to disk or logs.
  • What we store (only after a guardian links an account): the guardian's email and hashed password; the child's memorisation progress (lessons, reviews, streak, points); a nickname and avatar shown in the weekly league under the "league participation" consent; and, under the "teacher's circle" consent, the same nickname and progress for the teacher — never the real name, audio or the guardian's email.
  • What we never collect: birth date (age band only), real name outside the family, photos, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, any voiceprint. No Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer or ads.
  • Retention & deletion: no recordings are stored; a daily worker purges the sync-idempotency log older than 90 days and orphaned families. The guardian dashboard has a one-tap "delete the child's data permanently" that erases everything from the device and the server immediately and shows the deletion time. Deleting the account removes the whole family.
  • No user-to-user communication: no chat, comments, friends or audio sharing. All guardian screens sit behind a parental gate.

Contact / deletion requests: eng.m.natto@gmail.com
